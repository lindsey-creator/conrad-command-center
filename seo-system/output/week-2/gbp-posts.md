## Post 3 — Refinance: five questions worth asking first

- Suggested day: Wednesday
- Type: update
- Image: HUMAN_SUPPLIES
- Body:

**Thinking about refinancing in Cleveland or Northeast Ohio?** Before you apply, these five questions make the conversation useful:

1. **What is my goal?** Lower payment, shorter term, cash for a project, or “does this still make sense?”  
2. **How long will I keep this home?** Break-even timing matters.  
3. **What changed since I bought?** Income, credit, equity, or life plans.  
4. **What documents will I need?** A short prep list saves weeks of back-and-forth.  
5. **When is waiting the right answer?** Sometimes it is — and a good lender will say so.

We walk through refinance options honestly — **including when not to apply yet.**
