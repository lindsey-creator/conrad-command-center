## Post 3 — First-time buyer document checklist

- Suggested day: Wednesday
- Type: update
- Image: HUMAN_SUPPLIES
- Body:

First home in Greater Cleveland? You do not need a perfect file on day one — but having a few basics ready speeds up pre-approval:

- **ID and Social Security number** (for the application)  
- **Recent pay stubs or income documentation**  
- **Bank statements** (down payment and reserves)  
- **Tax returns** — often the last two years if self-employed or commission-heavy  
- **List of debts** — car loans, student loans, credit cards  

We will tell you exactly what applies to **your** situation — and explain anything that does not make sense.
