## FAQ snippets (optional on-page)

**What is the difference between pre-qualified and pre-approved?**  
They are not interchangeable labels. We explain what each status means for **your** file and what documents come next.

**When should a first-time buyer get pre-approved?**  
Usually before you tour homes — so you know your comfortable payment range and can move quickly when you find the right place.

**Do you quote rates on this page?**  
No. Rates change and every file is different. We start with goals, timeline, and documents.
