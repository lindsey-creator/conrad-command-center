## Thanksgiving-week readiness (refresh — for posts and page copy)

When borrowers compare Cleveland mortgage teams in **mid-November** — files targeting **Thanksgiving-week signing**, year-end refi, or winter move-ins — public reviews often reward lenders who make **document freshness, condition ownership, and written date updates** obvious:

1. **Document refresh before holiday-week signing** — pay stubs, insurance, and employer letters updated before underwriting or clear-to-close.
2. **Open conditions with clear owners** — borrower supplies vs. lender verifies, with due dates on the milestone map.
3. **Written updates when appraisal or inspection targets shift** — especially when dates slide into Thanksgiving week.
4. **Year-end refi calendar check** — timeline and documents before rate or payment talk.
5. **One accountable contact** when holiday calendars compress appraisal, inspection, and signing.
