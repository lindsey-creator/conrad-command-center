## FAQ snippets (optional on-page)

**What happens in the first few days after my offer is accepted?**  
Your loan file opens, document requests begin, and contract dates start driving milestones. Ask who owns your file and what to upload first.

**What are “conditions” on a mortgage file?**  
Items underwriting needs to finalize approval. The goal is a clear list, clear owners, and confirmation when each item is received.
