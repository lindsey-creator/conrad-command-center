## Post 3 — First purchase: pre-approval is not a blank check

- Suggested day: Wednesday
- Type: update
- Image: HUMAN_SUPPLIES (keys / first home — generic)

**Body:**

**First home in Cleveland or Northeast Ohio?** A **pre-approval letter** helps sellers take your offer seriously — but it is **not** a promise that every home or price point will fit **your** file.

Worth clarifying **before** you tour or write offers:

- What the letter is based on **today** (income, assets, credit snapshot)  
- What could change if your situation or the property type changes  
- How **conditions** and final underwriting still apply after you are under contract  

This is education, not a credit decision — every file is different — but you **do** deserve a team that explains **what the letter means** in plain English.
