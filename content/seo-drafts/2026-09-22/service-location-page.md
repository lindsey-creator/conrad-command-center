## FAQ (draft)

### What is the difference between pre-qualified and pre-approved?

**Pre-qualified** is often an early estimate based on information you share. **Pre-approved** usually means a lender has reviewed documents and underwriting factors more thoroughly — but neither is a final loan commitment. We explain what your letter means for **your** file.

### Who will I talk to after I apply?

You should know your **point person** and **when to expect updates** after application, after documents, and through underwriting — not “someone will call you.”

### What documents should I gather first?

It depends on your loan type and situation. We share a **checklist aligned to your file** so you are not scrambling when underwriting opens.

### Do you quote rates on this page?

No. Every situation is different. We focus on **process clarity** and honest timelines — not rate billboards.
