## FAQ (draft — verify before publish)

**What should I do in the first days after my offer is accepted?**  
Upload priority documents early, confirm your file is **received and assigned**, and ask how **earnest money** and inspection dates connect to loan milestones — in writing when possible.

**Who owns each condition on my file?**  
You should know what you supply vs. what the team verifies, and get confirmation when items are received — not only “in underwriting.”

**Can you guarantee my closing date before year-end?**  
No — every file is different. We focus on **honest timelines**, **title** and **holiday buffers**, and proactive updates when dates shift.
