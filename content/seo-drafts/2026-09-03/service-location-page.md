## What happens on your first call (education)

A useful first conversation — purchase or refinance — usually covers:

1. **Your goal and timeline** — buy, refi, or still deciding  
2. **Income and employment** — what documentation will likely apply  
3. **Credit and debt overview** — high level, not a lecture  
4. **Next step** — what to gather before a formal application  

No rate quotes on this page. Every situation is different.

## First-time buyer: pre-approval before house hunting

**Should you get pre-approved before you tour homes?** Usually yes:

- You learn a realistic budget before you fall in love with a listing  
- Your offer carries more weight when financing is documented  
- You discover early if something in your file needs time  

Pre-approval is a snapshot with named next steps — not a lifetime promise.
