## Pre-approval vs. pre-qualification (education)

**Pre-qualification** is often an early conversation about income, debts, and goals.

**Pre-approval** usually means a lender reviewed documents and credit within their process — so your budget picture is more defined before you write offers.

Ask any lender:

- What documents do you need for my pre-approval?  
- How long is it valid?  
- What could change before closing?

Every file is different.

## First call checklist (first-time buyers)

Have nearby:

1. **Monthly payment comfort** — a budget, not a rate  
2. **Employment history** — employer and tenure  
3. **Asset picture** — down payment and reserve accounts you may use  
4. **Written questions** — timeline, documents, who updates you  

You do not need perfect credit on day one. You do deserve clear next steps.

---

## Five questions before you choose a lender

1. Who is my point person after I apply?  
2. How will you update me on conditions and appraisal timing?  
3. When do we review cash-to-close — before signing day?  
4. What happens if my timeline slips?  
5. How do I reach you if something urgent comes up?
