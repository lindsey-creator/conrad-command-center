## Document checklist before you apply

A practical starter list for purchase or refinance files:

1. **Pay stubs or income proof** — recent, covering your current employment picture  
2. **W-2s and tax returns** — usually the last two years, depending on your file  
3. **Bank statements** — recent months, all pages if requested  
4. **Photo ID** — government-issued  
5. **Refi only:** recent mortgage statement and any home-value context you already have  

We will tell you **what applies to yours** — not a generic pile of paperwork.

## Refinance: five questions worth asking first

Before you apply for a refinance in Cleveland or Northeast Ohio:

1. What is my goal?  
2. How long will I keep this home?  
3. What changed since I bought?  
4. What documents will I need?  
5. When is waiting the right answer?

We walk through options honestly — including when not to apply yet.
