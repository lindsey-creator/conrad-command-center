**Body:**

After you apply, more than one person may work on your file — and that is normal. Confusion starts when **nobody explains the roles**.

A simple map many borrowers appreciate:

- **Loan officer / advisor** — your questions, timeline, and how program choices fit **your** goals  
- **Processor** — organizing documents, conditions, and handoffs to underwriting  
- **Underwriter** — reviewing the file against guidelines (you may not talk to them directly every day)
