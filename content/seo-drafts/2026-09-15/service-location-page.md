## FAQ (draft)

### What does “clear to close” mean?

It means underwriting and third-party steps required for your loan are complete enough to schedule closing — but the exact checklist depends on your file. We explain **what is done vs. still open** in plain language.

### When will I know my cash-to-close?

We aim to review **fees and cash-to-close before your signing appointment** — not as a surprise at the table. Every file is different; we will tell you what to expect for yours.

### How do you handle underwriting conditions?

We share **what we need, why we need it, and who is handling it** — aligned to the document checklist for your purchase or refi.

### Do you quote rates on this page?

No. Rates depend on credit, property, program, and market — **every situation is different**. Call **216-250-9078** or message us to talk through yours.
