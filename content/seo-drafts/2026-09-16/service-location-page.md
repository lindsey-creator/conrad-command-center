## FAQ (process + reputation)

### Who will I work with on my loan?

You get a **named point person** on your file — not a rotating call queue. If a handoff happens, we document who takes over and how to reach them.

### How do you handle underwriting conditions?

We track **open conditions with owners and due dates** — so closing week is not the first time you see a long list of surprises.

### What happens after appraisal is ordered?

We aim for **updates after order and after receipt** — and plain-language next steps if anything affects timing or value.

### What if my timeline or fees change?

We discuss changes **before** you sign — not as a surprise at the closing table. If something shifts, your point person explains what changed and why.

### Do you help first-time buyers in Cleveland?

Yes. We walk through pre-approval, documents, and closing costs in plain English — education, not pressure.

### What areas do you serve?

Greater Cleveland and Northeast Ohio — including Strongsville, Westlake, Solon, Akron, Canton, and surrounding communities.
